## ◉ What is LUMA//VOID?

LUMA//VOID is an open-source digital garden designed around one simple idea:

**Ideas should feel connected, not stored.**

Instead of presenting everything as ordinary folders and lists, LUMA//VOID turns your thoughts into a visual constellation.

Notes become nodes.

Relationships become connections.

Your knowledge becomes a living map.

---

## ✦ Vision

Most productivity tools are designed around organizing information.

LUMA//VOID is designed around **discovering relationships between information**.

The goal is to create a calm, beautiful space where you can:

- Capture ideas
- Write notes
- Connect related thoughts
- Explore your knowledge visually
- Search your personal universe
- Watch your digital garden grow

## ◈ Design language

LUMA//VOID uses a minimal futuristic visual language.

### Colors

| Color | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `#050509` | Void background |
| `#9B7CFF` | Primary violet |
| `#4DEEEA` | Cyan signal |
| `#B7FF4A` | Active state |
| `#FFFFFF` | Primary text |

The interface intentionally uses:

- Deep black backgrounds
- Soft gradients
- Neon accents
- Thin borders
- Monospace metadata
- Subtle motion
- Generous whitespace
